Professional Profile

About Me

I am Byakika Heziron Juma, an Electrical Engineer and Power Systems Researcher with professional experience in electrical infrastructure development, industrial power generation, engineering project supervision, and renewable energy systems.

I currently serve as an Electrical Engineering Officer at Mbarara City Council, Uganda. My work involves supervision of electrical infrastructure projects, inspection and maintenance of electrical installations, streetlighting systems, and technical support in engineering procurement and contract implementation.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Mbarara University of Science and Technology and a Postgraduate Diploma in Project Planning and Management from Uganda Management Institute.

I am also towards completion of a Master of Science in Power Systems Engineering at Makerere University, where my research focuses on hybrid renewable energy systems, advanced energy storage, and frequency stability.

My long-term professional interests include power systems research, renewable energy integration, advanced control systems, engineering project management, and contributing to engineering education and academic research.

Research & Publications

Master's research — Towards completion

Design and Optimal Control of a Hybrid Renewable Energy System with Advanced Storage for Frequency Stability

Makerere University

This research investigates frequency stability in an islanded hybrid renewable energy microgrid integrating solar PV, wind generation, battery energy storage, and supercapacitor energy storage.

The study involves mathematical modelling, MATLAB/Simulink simulations, Model Predictive Control, and performance evaluation under varying renewable generation and load conditions.
